Foodics RMS — Subscription Lifecycle States

Interactive demo summary — for design review

What this is

A small interactive demo showing how the Foodics POS (Cashier) and Console (Owner) experiences change as a merchant moves through 5 subscription lifecycle states — from healthy to fully blocked.

→ Open the live demo

Two experiences

🧾 Cashier (POS)

What the cashier sees while operating the till — banners, charge button, and lock screen.

🖥️ Console (Owner)

What the owner sees in the back office — banners, navigation, restricted overlays, billing CTAs, and an "Unblock" button.

Five states

Pre-notification — Full access. No banners, no warnings.
Pre-renewal — Payment approaching. Full access, with a countdown banner reminding the merchant to pay (pay if permitted, otherwise notify someone who can).
Grace period — Payment overdue. Full access continues, with a countdown banner before the grace period ends (pay if permitted, otherwise notify someone who can).
Soft block — Cashier keeps working under a "soft-block" warning treatment; Console is restricted to billing pages only.
Hard block — Cashier is fully locked out (can only notify the owner); Console is locked except for the License/payment page.

The "Unblock" button (Console only)

Shown only in Soft block / Hard block, in the restricted-access overlay. Its behavior changes depending on how many times it's been used:

  1. 1st click — restores full access for 5 days, then the button goes away until needed again.
  2. 2nd click — opens a support (SF) request to Team A; the button stays disabled until they unblock the account.
  3. 3rd click — opens a support (SF) request to Team B; the button stays disabled until they unblock the account.

How to explore it

On the live demo, use the left sidebar to switch between Cashier / Console and step through the 5 states. Click on banners, CTAs, and the lock-screen buttons to see the interactions and messages.
